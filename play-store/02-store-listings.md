## Customer app — English (India)

**App name (30 characters max)**  
Panditoo

**Short description (80 characters max)**  
Discover pandits and book puja services at a time and place that suits you.

**Full description (4,000 characters max)**

Panditoo helps you discover and request pandit services for pujas and Hindu ceremonies.

Choose a ceremony, review its details and estimated price, select your preferred date, time and service address, and send a booking request. Available pandits can respond, and you can follow the booking status from one place.

Key features:

- Browse pujas and ceremony details
- Find pandits serving your selected area
- Schedule a date and time
- Review pricing before confirming
- Pay supported booking amounts securely through the available payment provider
- Track upcoming and past bookings
- Use service OTPs for supported booking stages
- Rate your experience after a completed booking
- Use the app in English or Hindi

Availability, final pricing and provider acceptance may vary by location, date, ceremony requirements and provider availability. Panditoo is a booking marketplace and does not guarantee that every request will be accepted.

**App name (30 characters max)**  
Panditoo Partner

**Short description (80 characters max)**  
Manage puja requests, availability, bookings and earnings as a pandit partner.

**Full description (4,000 characters max)**

Panditoo Partner helps eligible pandits manage service requests and bookings from the Panditoo marketplace.

Create a partner profile, submit the requested verification information, set your service area and availability, and respond to suitable booking requests. Confirmed bookings, service details and earnings information are organized in one app.

Key features:

- Create and manage a pandit partner profile
- Add specializations, experience and service radius
- Submit identity information for verification
- Set available and unavailable dates
- Receive and respond to booking requests
- View booking details and service status
- Track credits, earnings and eligible withdrawals
- Receive booking notifications
- Use the app in English or Hindi

Registration does not guarantee approval, booking requests or earnings. Verification, availability, location, customer demand, applicable fees and marketplace rules affect access to opportunities. Credit, fee, payout and cancellation details are shown in the app and applicable terms.
