# Store asset and screenshot checklist

Create separate, accurate assets for each app. Do not include prices, rankings, “#1,” guaranteed income, guaranteed bookings, unverified testimonials, religious endorsement, Google Play badges, device frames that imply unsupported devices, or personal data.

## Required/typical assets

- [ ] High-resolution app icon: 512 × 512 px, 32-bit PNG, no rounded-corner mask baked in
- [ ] Feature graphic: 1024 × 500 px, JPG or 24-bit PNG (no alpha)
- [ ] At least 2 phone screenshots; prepare 6–8 portrait screenshots at consistent dimensions
- [ ] Optional 7-inch and 10-inch tablet screenshots only if tablets are supported and tested
- [ ] Optional preview video hosted on YouTube with no ads/claims that conflict with the listing
- [ ] In-app adaptive launcher icon foreground/background and monochrome icon tested on Android

Confirm current Play Console file/size requirements at upload time because Google may update them.

## Customer screenshot sequence

1. Home — “Find the right puja service”
2. Puja details — “Understand the ceremony and pricing”
3. Location/date selection — “Choose where and when”
4. Pandit options — “Review available pandits”
5. Booking confirmation — “Confirm with clear details”
6. Booking status — “Track every booking stage”
7. Ratings — “Share your experience”
8. Language selection — “Available in Hindi and English”

## Provider screenshot sequence

1. Requests — “See relevant booking requests”
2. Request details — “Review before responding”
3. Availability — “Set your working dates”
4. Confirmed booking — “Keep service details organized”
5. Earnings — “Track completed-service earnings”
6. Withdrawals — “View payout status”
7. Profile — “Manage service area and specializations”
8. Language — “Work in Hindi or English”

## Capture rules

- Use seeded fictional accounts, addresses, phone numbers, IDs and payments.
- Ensure screenshot content exactly matches the release build.
- Keep status bars, language, currency and dates internally consistent.
- Avoid showing real OTPs, complete bank details, identity documents or exact home addresses.
- Check legibility on the Play listing at thumbnail size.
- Localize screenshots if Hindi is a major acquisition channel.
