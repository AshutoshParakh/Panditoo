**Effective date:** `[EFFECTIVE DATE]`

This Privacy Policy explains how `[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]` (“Panditoo,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), located at `[REGISTERED ADDRESS]`, handles personal information when people use the Panditoo customer application, Panditoo Partner application, related websites, customer support, and marketplace services (together, the “Services”).

## 1. Information we collect

### Information you provide

- **Account and contact information:** name, mobile number, email address, preferred language, address, and referral/source information.
- **Booking information:** ceremony selected, requested date and time, service address, booking instructions, status, cancellation reason, and communications with support.
- **Provider profile and verification information:** specializations, years of experience, service radius, availability, identity-proof image/details, verification status, and profile information.
- **Payment and payout information:** payment order and transaction identifiers, payment status, amounts, refund information, bank-account holder name, bank name, account number, IFSC code, earnings, credits, withdrawals, and payout status. Payment card/UPI credentials are processed by the payment provider and are not intended to be stored by Panditoo.
- **Ratings and support content:** star ratings, comments, complaints, and information supplied in support requests.

### Information collected when you use the Services

- **Location:** precise or approximate foreground location when you choose to detect an address or service area; coordinates associated with a booking or provider service radius.
- **Photos and camera:** access to the camera or selected photos when a provider chooses to submit identity proof. We access only the image selected or captured for this purpose.
- **Notifications:** push token and notification preferences where notifications are enabled.
- **App, device and security information:** IP address, device/app version, operating system, timestamps, authentication records, logs, crash information, and security/anti-fraud signals, to the extent enabled in the production service.
- **Transaction and usage records:** bookings viewed or created, provider responses, service OTP stages, payments, credits, earnings, withdrawals, and feature interactions.

## 2. How we use information

We use information to:

- create, authenticate, maintain and secure accounts;
- match customers with providers and operate bookings;
- calculate prices, process payments, credits, refunds, earnings and payouts;
- verify providers and reduce fraud, misuse and safety risks;
- send OTPs, booking updates, transactional notifications and service messages;
- provide support, resolve disputes and enforce marketplace rules;
- improve reliability, accessibility and user experience;
- maintain records, comply with law, and establish or defend legal claims; and
- send promotional communications only where permitted and with available opt-out controls.

## 3. Permissions

Location, camera, photo-library and notification permissions are requested only when relevant features are used. You may deny or revoke them in Android settings. You can manually enter an address if location access is not required for the selected workflow. Denying a permission may prevent the related feature from working but should not block unrelated features.

## 4. How we disclose information

We may disclose information:

- **Between marketplace participants:** customers and confirmed/eligible providers receive the information reasonably necessary to evaluate and perform a booking, such as name, ceremony, time, service area/address, and booking status. Phone or full address should be limited until operationally necessary.
- **To processors and service providers:** hosting/database providers, SMS/OTP vendors, notification services, payment processor Razorpay, banking/payout partners, storage, security, analytics, crash reporting, and customer-support providers used in production.
- **For legal and safety reasons:** where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, the public, our rights, or the integrity of the Services.
- **For corporate transactions:** in connection with a merger, financing, acquisition, reorganization or sale, subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising unless this policy and the Play Data safety declaration are updated and any required consent is obtained.

## 5. Payment processing

Payments may be processed by Razorpay or another provider shown at checkout. The provider processes payment credentials under its own privacy policy. We receive transaction references, payment status, amount and limited payer information needed to reconcile transactions, issue refunds and prevent fraud.
