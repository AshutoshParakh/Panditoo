# Panditoo Support

Get help with sign-in, bookings, payments, refunds, provider verification, credits, earnings, withdrawals, privacy or account deletion.

**Customer support:** `[SUPPORT EMAIL]` / `[PHONE]`  
**Partner support:** `[PARTNER SUPPORT EMAIL]` / `[PHONE]`  
**Privacy and deletion:** `[PRIVACY EMAIL]`  
**Hours:** `[DAYS, HOURS, TIMEZONE]`  
**Typical response time:** `[TIME]`

When contacting support, include your booking reference and registered mobile number. Never send an OTP, full bank-account number, card details or identity document through ordinary email or chat.

### Common help topics

- OTP not received: confirm the mobile number and network, wait for the countdown, then request a new OTP. Never share it.
- Booking status: open Bookings and select the relevant reference.
- Cancellation/refund: open the booking to view the applicable rule, or contact support with the reference.
- Payment pending: do not repeatedly pay. Check the booking after a few minutes or contact support.
- Permission denied: enable only the needed permission in Android Settings → Apps → Panditoo/Panditoo Partner → Permissions.
- Delete account: use Profile/Account → Delete account or visit `[DELETION URL]`.
